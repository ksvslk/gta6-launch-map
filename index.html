    How to read the map

    Rockstar says GTA VI releases at midnight local time on 19 November in every region, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A country lights up when the game unlocks there.

    A solid country has a PlayStation Store listing that has unlocked. Regional store pages give an exact time for 63 countries and regions, in 15 waves. A country with several time zones unlocks all at once.

    A striped country has no store listing. It lights up once midnight passes in its capital. Treat that as a guide, because the game may not be sold there.

    This page follows the published schedule and your device's clock. It does not check the stores, and it covers digital copies only.

    Microsoft has not published country times for Xbox Series X|S. Xbox Cloud Gaming starts at 06:00 UTC on Thursday.

    Sources: Rockstar Games support and the PlayStation Store times compiled by GTA BOOM.
